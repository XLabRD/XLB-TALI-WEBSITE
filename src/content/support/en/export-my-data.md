Not from the app, at the moment. Export is not in the current development
pipeline, though it is a function we intend to include at some point. We would
rather say that plainly than imply a date we cannot stand behind.

## In the meantime, ask us

If you need your data, write to us and we will gladly send you a file with your
history. Tell us which zone or zones you want and roughly what period, and we
will put it together.

[Contact support](/support/#contact)

## Where this is going

History and statistics is a part of Tali we want to put real emphasis on, and
where we expect to add advanced features over time. Export belongs to that work
rather than sitting on its own.

How far back your history reaches in the app today depends on your plan — see
[my history doesn't go back very far](/support/missing-history/).
