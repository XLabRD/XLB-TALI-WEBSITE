To reset a Tali completely to its factory settings, follow these steps:

1. Unpair all Puks using the app.
2. Remove the Tali from your account.
3. Once it is unlinked, the Tali returns to its factory settings automatically.

There is no button to hold down on the device itself: removing it from your
account is what triggers the reset.

## What this means

The Tali is back to how it left the box, so you will need to set it up again
from the start — the Wi-Fi network, your Puks, and the zones with their
thresholds.
