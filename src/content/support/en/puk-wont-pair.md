Puk is Tali's wireless sensor. It connects over BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) and,
long as its reach is, it is not unlimited. Walls affect the distance at which it
can be detected.

## Symptom: Tali doesn't detect the Puk

Try setting it up right beside the Tali. If that works, the problem is the
distance between Tali and Puk — find a position that brings the two closer
together.

Remember too that one Tali holds up to ten Puks. If you are already at ten, the
next one has nowhere to go.

## Symptom: the battery's protective tab won't come out cleanly

On one of the earliest versions we found a manufacturing defect: the torque on
the screws prevents the plastic tab — the one that keeps the Puk from draining
its battery in transit — from being pulled free.

If you find that a Puk is not being detected, or that the plastic is only
partly out, you can open the Puk with Allen keys to remove the plastic and let
the battery make contact.

If you still have trouble, contact us and we will replace your Puk.
