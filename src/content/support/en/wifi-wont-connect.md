Tali acts as the hub that connects all your Puk sensors to Wi-Fi and uploads
your information to the cloud. The Wi-Fi connection matters not only for that,
but because it is also how firmware updates reach the device.

## Symptom: Tali doesn't detect the 5 GHz network

Tali only supports **2.4 GHz** networks. Some routers are configured to publish
5 GHz networks only, which Tali can neither detect nor join.

The fix is to configure your router — where it allows it — to also publish a
2.4 GHz network. Almost every router allows this. If yours does not, a router
that supports 2.4 GHz can be added for the connection.

## Symptom: Tali doesn't detect the network, and I do have 2.4 GHz

The router may simply be too far from the Tali. Try setting it up near your
router, and find a spot where the Tali is within reach of your network's signal.

## While it's offline

Nothing stops. The screen, the sensors and the local alerts do not depend on the
cloud, and the whole gap syncs once you are connected.
