Everything past the first frame on the screen happens through the app: putting
Tali on your network, pairing Puks, naming zones, setting thresholds and reading
history.

Download it, create your account, and keep that account — it is what your
devices, zones and history belong to. Signing in on a second phone or a tablet
shows you the same collection, not a new empty one.

## The QR shortcut

Every Tali screen carries a QR code that points at
[tali.my/hello](/hello) — the download page for both stores. If you are standing
in front of the device with a phone in your hand, that is the fastest route, and
it is also the one to send a family member who needs the app on their own phone.

## Before the next step

Have your Wi-Fi password ready, and be near the Tali with your phone. The
network is handed to the device over Bluetooth, so the two need to be in the
same room for the next step to work.
