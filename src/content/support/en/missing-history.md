Live readings, local alerts, presets and custom settings are Cloud Basic, and
they are yours for the life of the device at no cost. **Unlimited history**,
remote alerts and trend reports are Cloud Pro — included for two years with the
Founders Edition.

So a history that stops short is usually the tier, not a fault.

## It is not the outage

If your Wi-Fi went down, that gap is not why. Tali keeps measuring and storing
locally with no network at all, and when the connection returns the whole period
syncs — gap-free, in order, as though nothing happened.

## It is not a new phone either

Your devices, zones and history belong to your account, not your handset. Sign
in on a new phone and everything is there.

## If a specific stretch really is missing

That is worth reporting. Tell us the zone and the dates and we will look.
