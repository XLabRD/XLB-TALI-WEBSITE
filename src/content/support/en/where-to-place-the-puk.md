Put the Puk inside the space it is watching, close the door, and let it be. From
here the system runs itself: the screen cycles through your zones, the app shows
them live, and you only hear from Tali when something crosses a line you set.

## Where inside

- **Away from the door.** The air by an opening is the least representative air
  in the space.
- **Off the floor, out of the corner.** Mid-height, in open air, near the
  collection itself is what you want to know about.
- **Away from the cooling unit.** A sensor in the cold blast reads the machine,
  not the cellar.
- **Not buried.** Wedged behind bottles or under cedar, it measures a pocket.

## Give it time

A Puk that has just been moved is still reading the air it came from. Wait
before drawing conclusions — an hour in place tells you far more than the first
minute, and the first day tells you more than the hour.

## Range

Bluetooth reaches through wood and glass comfortably. Metal is what stops it: a
steel-doored wine fridge, a safe, a foil-lined cabinet. If a Puk reports from
the bench and goes quiet once the door closes, that door is the reason — move
the Tali closer rather than the Puk.
