---
title: How do I update Tali?
summary: You don't. Updates reach the Tali over Wi-Fi and the Puks over Bluetooth on their own.
section: fix
category: device

---

Tali and Puk keep gaining features and fixes after they are in your hands, and
the delivery is automatic. The cloud sends new firmware to your Tali over Wi-Fi;
the Tali passes updates to your Puks over Bluetooth. Most of it you will never
notice.

## The one time you are asked

Occasionally the app will ask you to keep it open for a moment while a Puk
updates. Puks are asleep almost all the time by design — that is how a coin cell
lasts a year and a half — so some updates need a nudge to catch them awake.
Leave the app on screen until it says it is done.

## If updates are not arriving

An update needs the Tali to be online. If the device has been off the network,
reconnect it and leave it alone for a while — it will catch up on its own
schedule rather than the moment you are watching.
