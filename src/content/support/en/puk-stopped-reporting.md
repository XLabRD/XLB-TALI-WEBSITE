A Puk that was reporting and now is not has two likely explanations, and you can
tell them apart in a minute.

## Range

Open the door and watch the app. If the Puk reappears within a few seconds, the
door is the problem, not the sensor.

Bluetooth passes through wood, glass and cabinetry without complaint. Metal is
what stops it — a steel-doored wine fridge, a safe, a foil-lined humidor. The
answer is to move the **Tali** closer to the zone, not to move the Puk out of
it. The Puk's job is to be inside.

## Battery

If it stays missing with the door open, check its age. The cell is a standard
CR2032 and lasts around eighteen months. Replacing it is a two-minute job you do
yourself — see [replacing the battery](/support/replace-puk-battery/).

## After a change of battery or place

The Puk rejoins on its own. If it does not appear within a minute, bring it
close to the Tali, let it reconnect there, then put it back.

## Still nothing

Tell us the zone, roughly when it went quiet and how old the battery is, and we
will pick it up from there.
