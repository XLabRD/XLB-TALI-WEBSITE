E-paper keeps showing whatever it drew last even with nothing plugged in. That
makes a frozen screen misleading: it looks like a running device that has
stopped thinking, when it is usually a device that has quietly lost power.

## Start with the cable

Check the USB-C cable at both ends, then try a different adapter, then a
different cable. A charger that is fine for a phone can still be a poor fit
here, and a cable that has been flexed at the connector for months is the single
most likely culprit in any powered device.

## If the screen is lit but the data looks old

Then the display is fine and the network is not. Tali keeps measuring and
showing readings locally whether or not it can reach the cloud — a stale
timestamp with live readings means the Wi-Fi dropped, and the fix is the network
one.

Nothing is lost while that lasts. When the connection returns the full history
syncs, gap-free.

## If it is dark and stays dark

A screen that is genuinely blank — not holding an old frame — with a known-good
cable and adapter is ours to sort out. Write to us and tell us what the screen
did last.
